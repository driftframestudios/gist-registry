# Visualization question routing

## Contract

- **What:** A compact view contract connecting the reader's operation, observation unit, variable roles, transformation, form, and exact-value companion.
- **When:** A new or materially revised visualization needs a selection decision.
- **Boundaries:** Source owners establish meaning and access; domain methods establish statistical warrants; the current task grants production and publication authority. A proposed form supplies a testable design choice rather than proof of the underlying claim.
- **Result:** One primary reading, a fitting form or table, its decisive alternative, and the observation needed to accept it.

## Fit and attention

- **Useful when:** The same records could become a time plot, annual panels, a map, a relationship plot, or a precise table.
- **Direct route when:** The comparison is settled and an isolated label needs correction.
- **Smallest useful dose:** One question, one population/unit, one variable-role table, one form decision, and one exact-value route.
- **May under-attend:** Domain validity, reader comprehension, accessibility, and final rendering each need their fitting observation.
- **Interactions:** Longitudinal observation and spatial support skills resolve their respective semantic branches. Transform fidelity and scientific composition receive the settled view contract. One destination owner retains the delivered artifact.

## Rules

### Rule: The reading job precedes the chart name

Name the operation and target: compare which units, follow whose change, locate
which pattern, inspect which distribution, or recover which value. Distinguish
exploration from explanation of an established finding. Three exact values may
earn a sentence or table. A missing identity, denominator, or time basis earns a
source question and a usable partial return.

### Rule: Meaning, calculation, and visual channel remain distinct

Record the observation unit, inclusion rule, measurement, unit, source standing,
and meaningful absence. Then name any grouping, weighting, normalization,
alignment, or model. Finally assign position, connection, color, panel, and
interaction roles. A color grouping supplies neither independent samples nor a
statistical adjustment. A field called `year` supplies no automatic time-series
question; a location label can be an ordinary category.

### Rule: Time receives the role the question needs

Time can supply elapsed position/order, a group such as year in a seasonal
overlay, a condition selecting annual panels, or complementary context such as
an aligned coverage strip. Declare these roles per view. Preserve unit identity
independently of time; distinguish calendar time, elapsed time, and visit order.
Load the [role guide](references/roles-and-evidence.md) when roles compete.

### Rule: Alternatives expose the deciding fact

Compare three to five materially different routes when the decision is
consequential. State what would favor each and select the smallest adequate
one. Shared scales serve magnitude comparisons; clearly labeled alternate
scales can serve another question. A second view earns space through a distinct
reading job. Production polish and evidence strength retain separate meanings.

### Rule: One admitted state supplies every carrier

Keep source, derived values, and display rounding separate. Figure, caption,
table, and export describe the same selected population and definitions. A
highlight preserves eligible records; a filter changes them explicitly. Preserve
observed zero, unavailable values, suppression, and partial coverage as distinct
states. State uncertainty type and method where it changes interpretation.

## Operating sequence

1. **Frame:** Name reader, question, consequence, and operation.
2. **Admit:** Establish source, counting unit, variables, identity, absence, and transformations.
3. **Assign roles:** Separate data meaning, statistical operation, visual channel, and reader task.
4. **Compare:** Choose a primary form, a meaningful alternative, and the fact that changes the choice.
5. **Transfer:** Supply values, source, labels, scales, uncertainty, companion, and named destination owner.
6. **Observe:** Check arithmetic and semantic agreement; inspect actual rendering when produced. Keep comprehension claims tied to actual reader evidence.

## Completion and re-entry

The receiver can identify the population, perform the named comparison, recover
exact values, and explain why the form fits. Unresolved source meaning remains
with a named owner and a concrete next observation. A changed question,
population, field meaning, or demonstrated misreading reopens the decision.
