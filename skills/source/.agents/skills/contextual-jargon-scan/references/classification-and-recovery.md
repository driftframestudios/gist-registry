# Classification and recovery across representations

Use this guide when the same term appears in reader prose and in a source-owned
identifier, or when several representations serve different audiences. The
task is to preserve the distinctions readers need while removing unnecessary
decoding work.

## One occurrence record

Keep the smallest record that changes a revision decision:

| Field | Useful content |
| --- | --- |
| Passage and reader task | Enough surrounding prose to recover the intended action. |
| Meaning owner | Ordinary language, a technical definition, quoted source, schema, or local metaphor. |
| Missing distinction | The object, actor, mechanism, unit, state, or consequence the reader must infer. |
| Disposition | Retain, define, revise, or return to the named owner. |
| Receiving observation | Read the revised passage in context; check the corresponding rendered or generated representation when it changes. |

The record can remain in the existing review comment. A separate glossary or
inventory earns its place when another reader will use it.

## Cases that change the action

**A governed key and its explanation.** A manifest's `technicalVeracity` key
retains its exact spelling and schema meaning. Its adjacent explanation can
name which source inspection or execution supports the artifact. Replacing the
key itself creates a schema change and belongs with that owner.

**A useful metaphor with an absent referent.** “The handoff preserves the
boundary” leaves the object and actor unspecified. “The author supplies the
accepted source version; the receiving repository owns its runtime checks”
makes the relationship inspectable. If “boundary” names an already defined
serialization policy, retaining the term can be the clearer choice.

**One term with multiple valid meanings.** “State” may mean a process object's
current contents, database rows, or an artifact's publication standing. Define
the specific referent near each use. A global replacement would erase a useful
technical distinction.

**A disputed definition.** Preserve the quoted or governed form, state the
reader's difficulty, and return the question to its owner. Independently clear
prose can proceed while that single occurrence remains unresolved.

## Recovery and verification

Compare the before/after entailment: who acts, what changes, under which
conditions, and what follows? If the replacement adds an unsupported promise
or removes a necessary qualifier, restore the passage and choose a smaller
definition. Re-read the surrounding paragraph and its linked continuation.
For generated copy, confirm source-to-projection agreement through the existing
builder; for an untouched source quote, a fidelity comparison is sufficient.

These are authored classification cases, not results from a controlled reader
study. Natural receiving use can establish a local improvement and identify
where a different audience needs another definition. Broad comprehension or
effectiveness claims require their own observation.
