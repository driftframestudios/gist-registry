# Scientific figure composition

## Contract

- **What:** Composes scientifically faithful figures with deliberate visual
hierarchy, panel geometry, semantic color, annotation, and usable final delivery.

- **When:** A correct plot needs a coherent paper, poster, slide, report, or web
composition; dense panels or presentation quality affect a real reading task.

- **Result:** A figure specification and inspected artifact at its actual
receiving size, preserving data identity, uncertainty, and exact companions.

- **Boundaries:** The skill supplies purposeful aesthetics and a reviewable
composition, not a quantified beauty score or a guarantee of comprehension,
trust, persuasion, scientific validity, or complete accessibility. Exact font
sizes and contrast/delivery requirements come from the named receiver.
- **Interactions:** A settled reader question and view contract supply the input.
  `visualization-question-routing` can help select that contract; direct
  reasoning is sufficient when it is already clear. The domain, scale, and
  composition skills each own their named result and can be used separately.

## Fit and recovery

Use the smallest branch that changes the receiving figure. Existing source,
analysis, rendering, and document owners retain their roles. Supply the figure's
question, source/quantity, relevant parameters, and delivery target in ordinary
prose; a new manifest is optional. A missing identity, unit, model assumption,
or publication grant becomes a named held decision with a useful descriptive
view or narrower admitted subset where fitting. Resume when that owner supplies
the missing input.

## Rules

- Give the figure a reading sequence: question/context, primary comparison,
  qualification, and recovery path. An explanatory title states a supported
  finding; an exploratory title leaves the question open. Choose an overview,
  aligned panels, or detail inset from that reading rather than filling a grid.
- Define panel roles and repeated geometric relationships. Shared baselines,
  scales, axes, order, and semantic colors make comparisons easier to locate;
  distinct quantities keep visible labels and their own units. Give dense
  evidence enough area, and keep panel labels and caption references stable.
- Use typography and spacing as a hierarchy: legible primary labels, subordinate
  method notes, consistent alignment, and enough separation to group related
  elements. Keep scientific symbols, minus signs, superscripts, and units
  intact. More white space is useful when it clarifies grouping; sparse evidence
  is not the same thing as a clearer figure.
- Assign color by meaning: sequential magnitude, divergence around a declared
  reference, cyclic variables, or unordered identity. Define one palette/domain
  policy per comparable family; include over/under/missing states. Selective
  emphasis points to supported features while retaining contrary observations,
  denominators, uncertainty, and comparison context. Provide shape/line/text
  routes when color carries a necessary distinction.
- Render data marks with deliberate weight and scaffolding. Keep gridlines,
  baselines, direct labels, or boundary cues that serve the reading. Flat
  quantitative encodings are a useful default; embellishment earns its space
  through a named explanatory role. A blanket aesthetic ban would overstate
  the evidence and discard useful domain diagrams.
- Separate overview from detail by disclosure, readable local scrolling, or
  full-size access on web. Essential claim qualifiers remain visible. Preserve
  selected-state agreement across marks, table, caption, and export.
- Fit the artifact to its medium: physical placed size for papers/reports,
  viewing distance and exposure time for slides/posters, container/keyboard
  behavior for web. Build from one admitted value/identity source; destination
  renderers may compose differently. Rasterize dense layers when measured file
  size/render cost warrants it while keeping useful labels vector/searchable.
- Inspect the artifact at final size with longest labels and meaningful extreme
  states. Arithmetic/identity tests, rendered legibility, accessibility, source
  interpretation, and publication authority retain separate observations.
  Richer F0–F3 treatment increases production commitment; it does not promote
  evidentiary standing.

## Completion and evidence

The receiving figure and companion agree on the admitted meaning. The changed
boundaries have proportionate semantic checks and, for an actual figure, a
rendered observation at its receiving size. Return the choice, relevant source,
observed behavior, coverage boundary, and any remaining scientific or delivery
decision in the existing work record.

This package is a research-informed instruction contract with authored synthetic
exercises. Running an exercise establishes its explicit assertions and outputs;
independent review, audience comprehension, assay suitability, accessibility,
