# Probe-bearing exemplar formation

## Contract

- **What:** A first-version route from one consequential technical question to
  a self-contained, source-grounded registry artifact.
- **When:** A public-safe sample or probe has a named reader, a changed ability,
  and enough source or behavior evidence to support a reviewable first
  coordinate.
- **Boundaries:** Source owners and focused observations retain evidence
  authority. Architectural selection remains with
  `architecture-convergence-vantages`; material strengthening of an accepted
  coordinate remains with `versioned-exemplar-successor`. Repository acceptance,
  immutable tags, Pages delivery, and optional Gist publication retain their
  named review and human authority.
- **Result:** One review-ready artifact version whose practical question,
  representations, provenance, technical-veracity standing, focused evidence,
  instruction yield, transfer boundary, recovery route, and next receiver
  remain inspectable.

## Fit and attention

- **Useful when:** A code sample, probe, or synthetic domain example can support
  another collaborator once it has a stable first identity and enough local
  context to travel.
- **Poor fit when:** One scoped repair completes the work, several mechanisms
  still create different guarantees, or an accepted artifact needs a material
  successor.
- **Smallest useful dose:** One versioned artifact, the focused checks that
  establish its present claim, and only the representations needed by a named
  reader.
- **Foregrounds:** Artifact granularity, source authority, material evidence,
  technical-veracity standing, standalone hydration, and the distinction
  between acceptance and publication.
- **Interactions:** `architecture-convergence-vantages` can select a mechanism
  before formation. `versioned-exemplar-successor` can receive a later material
  strengthening after the first coordinate is accepted.
- **Rest or release when:** Source standing, the distinguishing observation, or
  the receiving use remains capable of changing the artifact's honest shape.
  Keep the candidate in planning with the exact evidence or authority event that
  reopens formation.

## Rules

### Rule: The practical question leads

Name one practical question, the reader it affects, and the changed ability the
artifact seeks. The artifact kind, selected code, and review surfaces follow
from that receiving use.

### Rule: Granularity is part of the technical claim

Select the smallest representation that demonstrates the intended mechanism
with its material state, boundary, and recovery relationships intact. State
what the selection establishes and which adjacent behavior remains with the
complete artifact or another receiver.

### Rule: Runnable source and cited owners retain authority

Implementation claims resolve to runnable source and focused observations.
Exact excerpts resolve byte-for-byte to declared owners. A proposed mechanism
uses `draft_probe` or another fitting technical-veracity standing until the
distinguishing observation exists.

### Rule: Material checks connect behavior, contrast, oracle, and consequence

For runnable material, the focused check set connects the consumer behavior, a
material contrast, boundary condition, or plausible fault, the observable
result that distinguishes the desired state, and why the distinction matters.
Counts, coverage, workflow runs, and digests support only the scoped facts they
observe.

### Rule: Conditional technical review follows the artifact shape

Review complexity and allocation when collection passes, lookup shape,
intermediate material, ordering, or resource placement can change the
guarantee. Review conditional decision topology when branching expresses a
single discriminant, additive rules, mutually exclusive state, extension
strategies, or retained state. Record local measurement where source reasoning
leaves runtime behavior open. When this review reveals competing mechanisms
with materially different guarantees, formation rests and
`architecture-convergence-vantages` receives the decision.

### Rule: Veracity attaches to claims and evidence

Use the current schema's technical-veracity values. Answer ranges or levels
enter only when they change a receiving decision, and they describe artifact
or answer depth. A probability names the event, population, horizon,
conditions, and evidence it estimates.

### Rule: Independently addressable representations hydrate independently

Give each representation enough local question, source relation, evidence
standing, and complete-artifact route to remain intelligible when copied or
linked alone. Purposeful repetition stays when it supplies that standalone
context.

### Rule: Instruction yield carries the changed ability forward

Translate the observed mechanism into one reusable implementation, review, or
recovery instruction. The instruction states the desired behavior, stays
within the artifact's evidence boundary, and points to local measurement where
the receiving environment owns the answer.

### Rule: Semantic markers remain optional and text-only

Use `TARGET`, `CONTRAST`, and `GUARD` when they make desired, diagnostic, and
protective behavior easier to retrieve. Desired-state comments lead;
diagnostic material stays clearly marked as comparison.

### Rule: First-coordinate standing stays distinct from release

Artifact standing, source acceptance, immutable tagging, Pages delivery, and
optional Gist publication each retain their own coordinate and receiver. A
review-ready first version can complete formation while later release actions
remain human-held.

## Operating sequence

1. **Reconstruct source:** Confirm the Issue, branch, public-safe source owner,
   current behavior, evidence standing, license, and publication boundary.
2. **Name the receiving use:** State the practical question, reader, changed
   ability, and consequence that makes a stable coordinate useful.
3. **Choose the artifact shape:** Select `artifactKind`, version, granularity,
   representations, execution surface, and any bounded extension fields.
4. **Bind authority:** Record provenance class, public-safe source keys,
   technical-veracity standing, relationships, and exact derivation where
   applicable.
5. **Form material evidence:** Add focused behavior checks, expected results,
   contrast or failure observations, recovery evidence, and conditional
   complexity or decision-topology notes when they change the claim.
6. **Prepare standalone reading:** Make the README and every independently
   addressable representation intelligible with its complete-artifact route,
   then carry the changed ability into one bounded instruction yield.
7. **Validate:** Run the nearest artifact checks, registry and schema suite,
   public-boundary checks, and `git diff --check`.
8. **Return for review:** Name the current standing, evidence reach, transfer
   boundary, recovery move, and next receiver. Tagging and projection begin
   only through their accepted routes.

## Completion

Formation completes when a reviewer can identify the artifact, inspect or run
the behavior it carries, understand the material contrast and observable
evidence, trace every claim to its owner, apply the bounded instruction yield,
and continue through one named next receiver. A materially different artifact,
an observed near miss, or repeated formation mechanics can revise the skill or
earn a deterministic helper.
